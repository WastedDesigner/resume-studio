Save as PDF

Your browser's print window will open. To get a clean PDF:

  1. Set Destination (or Printer) to Save as PDF.
  2. Keep Margins on Default.
  3. Set Scale to 100% and enable Background graphics under More settings to preserve the layout and colours.
  4. Untick Headers and footers under More settings.
  5. Click Save.
Don't choose Microsoft Print to PDF or another printer. Those can turn your text into shapes, so it can't be selected, searched or read by applicant tracking systems. Use Save as PDF (Save to PDF in Firefox).

Save as PDF keeps real, selectable text, so recruiters and applicant tracking systems can read and search it.

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Resume Studio runs entirely in your web browser. There is no account, no server that receives your resume, and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we never see what you type.

Where your resume is kept

In your browser's storage on this device, for this website only. It isn't synced to other devices or browsers. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes it, so download a backup from Files now and then.

Files you download or import

PDF, Word and other downloads are created on your device and saved where you choose. Files and backup codes you import are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Other services this page contacts

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces when you use them. Like any web request, Google receives your IP address and browser details, but not your resume.
  • cdnjs (run by Cloudflare) supplies the free JSZip library the first time you download a Word file.
  • jsDelivr supplies copies of your chosen Google typefaces when you download a Word file, so they can be built into the file and look right on computers that don't have them. Only the typeface names are requested, never your resume.
  • Website hosting may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Links on your resume are only opened if you click them.

Your control

Delete one resume, or every resume, in Files → Privacy. Removing this site's data in your browser settings also deletes everything.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
